=== HARAMBEE CASH STATIC FILES ===

To add your own images:
1. Replace piclog.png with your logo (recommended: 200x200 or larger, PNG format)
2. Replace apple-touch-icon.png with your icon (recommended: 180x180, PNG)
3. Replace favicon.ico with your favicon (recommended: 32x32 or 64x64, ICO format)
4. Replace icons/icon-192x192.png with your PWA icon (192x192, PNG)
5. Replace icons/icon-512x512.png with your PWA icon (512x512, PNG)

Sounds:
- game-end.wav is already generated as a simple beep
- To add custom sounds, replace game-end.wav with your own

PWA Icons:
- Both icons are required for PWA installation
- Use maskable icons for best results
