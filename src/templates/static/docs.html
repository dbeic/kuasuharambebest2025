Harambee Cash Documentation

Overview

Harambee Cash is a web-based gaming platform where users participate in periodic games with random winner selection. The platform features user registration, wallet management, game participation, and administrative controls.

Current System Specifications

  • Minimum Stake: Ksh. 10.00 per game entry
  • Minimum Players: 2 players required to start a game
  • Game Interval: 30 seconds per game cycle
  • Platform Fee: 10% deducted from the total prize pool
  • Winner Share: 90% of the prize pool awarded to the winner
  • Queue Management: Players queue until 2 + unlimited are accumulated, then game executes
  • Rescheduling: Games reschedule every 30 seconds until the 2 + -player threshold is met
  • Withdrawal Eligibility: Only winnings from games are eligible for withdrawal
  • Minimum Withdrawal: Ksh. 100.00
  • Minimum Deposit: Ksh. 50.00
  • Loan System: New users receive a KES 200.00 welcome loan. Automatic repayment of KES 100.00 occurs when win earnings reach KES 100.00.

Key Features

  • User Registration & Login: Secure registration with email, username, and password.
  • Wallet Management: Users can view balances; admins can process deposits and withdrawals.
  • Loan Management: Automatic loan creation, repayment tracking, and withdrawal eligibility based on loan status.
  • Game Logic: 30-second game cycles with random winner selection from queued players.
  • Admin Dashboard: Comprehensive admin controls for user management and financial oversight.
  • Transaction History: Complete audit trail of all financial transactions.
  • Real-time Updates: Live game status and queue information.

How to Play

  1. Register: Create an account with your email and username.
  2. Deposit: Fund your wallet with a minimum of Ksh. 50.00.
  3. Join Game: Click the Play button to join the queue with a Ksh. 10.00 stake.
  4. Wait for Players: The game waits until 2 + players are in the queue.
  5. Game Execution: When 2 + players accumulate, the game runs and a winner is selected.
  6. Win: The winner receives 90% of the prize pool in their wallet.
  7. Withdraw: You can withdraw your winnings (minimum Ksh. 100.00).
  8. Loan Repayment: Your winnings automatically repay your welcome loan of KES 200.00 when you reach KES 100.00 in win earnings.

Loan System Details

  • Loan Creation: Every new user receives an automatic welcome loan of KES 200.00 upon registration.
  • Loan Repayment: When your win earnings reach KES 100.00, a repayment of KES 100.00 is automatically triggered.
  • Repayment Process: The repayment is deducted from your win earnings and applied to your loan balance.
  • Loan Completion: Your loan is considered fully repaid when your remaining balance reaches KES 0.00.
  • Withdrawal Requirement: You must have fully repaid your loan before you can request any withdrawals.
  • Loan Tracking: Your loan status is updated in real-time and visible in your account dashboard.
  • Multiple Repayments: Multiple KES 100.00 repayments may be processed until the loan is fully repaid.
  • House Account: All loan repayments are deposited into the platform's house account to support operations.
