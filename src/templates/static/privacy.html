Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 19th July 2026

At Harambee Cash, your privacy is a top priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you interact with our platform, in compliance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and applicable international regulations.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Such as your email address, username, and password during registration.
  • Financial Information: Including your wallet balance, transaction history, deposits, and withdrawal records.
  • Loan Information: Including loan amounts, repayment status, repayment history, and loan balance tracking.
  • Usage Data: Such as login timestamps, game participation history, IP addresses, device information, and browser details.
  • Communication Data: Any correspondence you have with our support team.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Platform Operation: To operate, maintain, and improve the platform experience and gameplay functionality.
  • Financial Processing: To process payments, update wallet balances, manage deposits and withdrawals, and maintain accurate transaction records.
  • Game Management: To manage game participation, select winners, and distribute prizes according to our game rules.
  • Loan Management: To manage the loan system, including loan creation, automatic repayment from winnings, and tracking of loan status.
  • Communication: To communicate with you about updates, support inquiries, security alerts, and promotional offers (with your consent).
  • Security: To detect, prevent, and address fraud, security breaches, and other technical issues.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with legal obligations and regulatory requirements.

3. Data Security

  • Encryption: All sensitive data is encrypted using industry-standard protocols (SSL/TLS) during transmission.
  • Password Protection: Passwords are hashed and salted using bcrypt and are not accessible to anyone, including our team.
  • Access Control: Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel only, on a need-to-know basis.
  • Regular Audits: We conduct regular security audits to ensure your data remains protected.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties unless required by law, with your explicit consent, or as necessary to provide services through trusted partners who comply with our privacy standards.

  • Service Providers: We may share data with payment processors and hosting providers who assist in operating our platform.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or government regulation.
  • Investor Reporting: We may share anonymized loan performance data with our investors for financial reporting purposes.

5. Data Retention

  • We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period thereafter to comply with legal obligations.
  • You may request the deletion of your account and associated data at any time, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Transaction records are retained for a minimum of 5 years for financial audit and compliance purposes.

6. Your Rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of your personal data we hold.
  • Right to Rectification: You may correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request deletion of your data, subject to legal requirements.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You may ask us to limit how we use your data.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object: You may object to certain data processing activities.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your session and improve your experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect platform functionality.

8. Children's Privacy

Our platform is not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we discover such data, it will be immediately deleted.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated via email or platform notification. Continued use of our platform constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
